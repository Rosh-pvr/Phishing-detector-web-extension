DOMAIN SCORE GIVE AS score in the PHISH_DETECTOR.


In each analysis the task looks up the domain’s creation date (via WHOIS). It then converts that age into a “domain age score” that feeds the overall risk score:

If WHOIS has no creation date → score 20 (treated as suspicious).
Age < 30 days → score 30 (very risky).
Age between 30 days and 1 year → score 15 (moderately risky).
Age ≥ 1 year → score 0 (no penalty).
So the younger or unknown the domain, the higher the score, increasing odds of a PHISHING verdict.
